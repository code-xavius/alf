# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and give actionable feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Accept that maintainers may decline changes that do not fit the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory comments.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers. Reports are reviewed
confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem
inappropriate.

This document is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
